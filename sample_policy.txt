BgvDev Employee Handbook 2026

Annual leave: Every full-time employee at BgvDev gets 27 days of paid annual leave per year.
Unused leave up to 5 days can be carried over to the next year, but it must be used before March 31.

Work from home: Employees may work from home on Mondays and Fridays. The office in Ahmedabad is
open from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM. The team lunch happens every second Thursday at Café Zorba.

Equipment: Every new joiner receives a laptop and a mechanical keyboard called the "Thunderkey 88".
Laptops must be returned within 7 days after the last working day.

Learning budget: Each employee has a yearly learning budget of 18,000 INR for books and courses.
Requests must be approved by the team lead named Priya Raman.
